## Script 1 — Rosharon: Sierra Vista
**Angle:** I live here. Resident perspective only I can give.
**Filming:** Sat AM · Sierra Vista entrance · county roads · open land backdrop
**Verify night before:** Flood zone per FEMA map for Sierra Vista specifically · current home price range on MLS

---

### HOOK — First 3 Seconds (On Camera, Sierra Vista Entrance Monument)

> "I am not just your realtor for Rosharon. I actually live here."

*Step toward camera. Pause. Let it land.*

---

### MAIN CONTENT (On Camera + B-Roll)

**[ON CAMERA — standing at entrance or open field]**

> "Welcome to Sierra Vista in Rosharon, Texas. And I want to be upfront with you from the start — I am not giving you the brochure version of this neighborhood. I live here. My family lives here. So everything I'm about to tell you is the real thing."

**[B-ROLL: slow pan of Sierra Vista entrance monument, wide shot of community]**

**[ON CAMERA — walking toward camera on community road]**

> "Here is the first thing that surprises every buyer who looks out here. My address says Rosharon. But when I pull up my city in the county records, it says Iowa Colony. That is not a typo. That is actually how jurisdiction works in this part of Brazoria County. And it matters — because your property taxes, your HOA district, and your utility service areas can all hinge on that distinction. That is your first lesson for today. Free of charge."

*Light laugh. Natural.*

**[B-ROLL: county road drive-by, open acreage on FM 1462 or CR 56-57, contrast shot of new construction rising beside open land]**

**[ON CAMERA — open land backdrop, facing camera]**

> "Now here is what I love most about this area, and I mean genuinely love it as someone who chose to live here. You still have two worlds out here. You have the master-planned side — think Sierra Vista, Sterling Lakes — neighborhoods with community pools, HOAs, and walkable streets. And then you have the rural side right around the corner. Actual acreage. Land. If you have ever dreamed about owning land in Texas — not just a yard, actual land — this corridor is where that becomes affordable and real."

**[B-ROLL: open acreage shots, land for sale signs if visible from public road, contrast between neighborhood streets and open fields]**

**[ON CAMERA — teacher moment, relaxed energy]**

> "And since we are talking about land, I have to give you the land buyer checklist right now. When you are buying acreage in Texas there are five things you need to know before you make an offer. Number one: water. Does this property have a water well, a rural water supply connection, or nothing? Number two: septic. Is there a system already installed or will you be starting from zero? Number three: road access. Does the property have frontage on a maintained road or do you need an easement? Number four: mineral rights. In Texas, surface and mineral rights are often separated — make sure you know exactly what you are buying. Number five: flood zone. And I want to be very clear here — never assume. Every address is different. You check the FEMA Map Service Center by the specific address before you make any decisions. Sierra Vista and the community right next door can be in completely different flood zones. I have seen it. Always verify."

**[B-ROLL: wide open land shots, close-up of land marker, peaceful morning light]**

**[ON CAMERA — standing in neighborhood, genuine resident energy]**

> "One more thing I want you to know as someone who actually lives out here. My nearest grocery store is the H-E-B in Manvel. And I want you to think about that for a second. A brand new H-E-B opened close enough to be MY store. That tells you everything about the direction this corridor is heading. Infrastructure follows people. And people are moving out here."

## Script 4 — Pearland: Shadow Creek Ranch
**Angle:** Established suburb — it is all already here. No waiting.
**Filming:** Sat AM · Pearland Town Center · Shadow Creek Ranch entrance + lakes · Nature Trail
**Verify night before:** Nature Trail public access confirmed · Shadow Creek Ranch HOA rec centers are private (exterior only) · current home price range on MLS**

---

### HOOK — First 3 Seconds (On Camera, Shadow Creek Ranch lake or Nature Trail)

> "What if you could move to a neighborhood where everything you need is already built and has been for decades?"

*Gesture at the surroundings. Let the environment speak.*

---

### MAIN CONTENT (On Camera + B-Roll)

**[ON CAMERA — Pearland Town Center, morning before crowds, OR Shadow Creek Ranch entrance]**

> "Welcome to Pearland, Texas. I am Kareesa Gonzales with Keller Williams Preferred, and I want to tell you something about Pearland that I think gets overlooked in all the conversation about the 288 corridor. When people talk about Rosharon and Iowa Colony and Manvel, those are growth stories. They are exciting, they are real, and I love them. But Pearland is a different kind of story. Pearland is the established chapter. The one where you move in and everything is already here. The schools. The hospitals. The shopping. The parks. All of it. You are not waiting for the H-E-B to open. The H-E-B has been there."

**[B-ROLL: Pearland Town Center open-air mall — morning, before crowds, wide establisher shot, storefronts, open-air atmosphere]**

**[ON CAMERA — walking near Shadow Creek Ranch entrance or lake view from public street]**

> "And if we are talking about where to live in Pearland, we have to talk about Shadow Creek Ranch. This community has been here since the late 1990s. We are talking about 17 lakes. 150 acres of parks and greenbelts. Trails that actually connect. This is what a master-planned community looks like after it matures. It has had time to grow into itself, and you can feel it."

**[B-ROLL: Shadow Creek Ranch entrance monument, lake views from public streets, greenbelts, walking paths visible from public access, mature trees]**

**[ON CAMERA — Shadow Creek Ranch Nature Trail walking and talking, natural outdoor backdrop]**

> "I want to walk with you here for a second. This is the Shadow Creek Ranch Nature Trail, and it is one of my favorite spots in Pearland because it genuinely does not feel like you are in a suburb. There is a wetlands viewing deck out here. You can hear birds. You can stop and just breathe for a minute. And this is still inside a Houston suburb that puts you within 20 to 25 minutes of downtown Houston on a good traffic day. That combination — nature access plus city proximity — is genuinely hard to find at this price point anywhere in Texas."

**[B-ROLL: Nature Trail walk-and-talk — walking toward camera down trail, wetlands deck views, birds if visible, green natural surroundings, peaceful]**

**[ON CAMERA — near PearScape sculpture or fun local landmark]**

> "Okay I need to show you one more thing because this makes me smile every time. Pearland takes their name seriously. These are the PearScape sculptures — giant pear installations scattered around the city. And if you are moving to Pearland and you do not know about these yet, your new neighbors are absolutely going to take you on a tour. It is a whole thing. It is charming and it is very Pearland. And honestly that is the best way I can describe this city. It is established, it is well-run, and it has a personality. That is rare."

**[B-ROLL: PearScape sculptures — fun close-up, Kareesa next to one smiling, driving-by shot of others, engagement-worthy visual]**

**[ON CAMERA — back near lakes or Nature Trail, genuine energy]**

> "Here is the honest truth about Pearland. If your priority is having it all figured out before you move in, Pearland is your answer. Great schools — verify your specific school zone with Pearland ISD directly because zones do get updated. Medical center access. Retail and dining at a real town center not just a strip mall. A nature trail in your own neighborhood. And a commute that keeps you connected to Houston without putting you an hour away from everything."

> "Now, the trade-off, and I always tell my buyers both sides. Pearland home prices reflect all of this. The infrastructure, the maturity, the demand. You are going to pay more per square foot here than you would further south on the corridor. That is not a bad thing. It is a different investment thesis. Some buyers want to buy early and ride the growth. Some buyers want to buy into something that is already proven. Pearland is the proven play."
